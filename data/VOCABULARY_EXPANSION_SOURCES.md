# Vocabulary expansion sources

The reviewed expansion in `vocabulary-expansion-reviewed.json` uses the
public-domain **ejdic-hand** English–Japanese dictionary as a neutral definition
reference. The data was accessed through `harurow-ejdict` 2.2.0.

- Upstream data: <https://github.com/kujirahand/EJDict>
- JavaScript package: <https://github.com/Harurow/EJDict>
- License: public domain

Definitions were normalized for this project and checked against the part of
speech and use in the play. Missing, inflected, or incorrectly lemmatized items
were manually corrected. Play-specific clarification remains in the optional
`inThisPlay` field rather than the neutral dictionary meaning.
