6. STRUCTURAL UNIT TYPES FOR 002
============================================================
DIALOGUE
- Spoken text associated with one canonical speaker ID.
- May carry attached delivery/action cues; cues themselves are not spoken Sentence IDs.

STAGE_DIRECTION
- Dramatic action, movement, entrance/exit, lighting, sound, curtain, prop, or blocking direction.

SCENE_DESCRIPTION
- Scene-opening location/time/set/state prose.

SOUND_OR_MUSIC_CUE
- Distinct non-dialogue sound/music event when separately represented.

CURTAIN_MARKER
- CURTAIN / QUICK CURTAIN or equivalent explicit scene-ending marker.

STRUCTURAL_HEADING
- Act/Scene/source scene-setting heading represented by scene metadata.
