UNIT -> SENTENCE RULE
For a dialogue row with spoken_sentence_count K:
SEN-{UNIT_ID}-01 through SEN-{UNIT_ID}-{K:02d}

PREAMBLE, stage/context material and CURTAIN/QUICK_CURTAIN receive no spoken Sentence ID.

SENTENCE -> CHUNK RULE

The exact chunk counts and character spans are stored in the applicable 003 data shard.
