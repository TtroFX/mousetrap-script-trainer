AUTHORITY AND EXTRACTION RULES
- The source PDF is the sole textual authority.
- Full verbatim script text is not duplicated in this public-repository metadata material.
- Speaker boundaries are detected from PDF source layout/style as well as textual punctuation; a missing colon does not erase a visually explicit speaker label.
- A source speaker span may contain bracketed stage/action directions. Those directions remain source-context material but are excluded from spoken-sentence IDs.
- Scene-setting prose, movement/action cues, sound/lighting cues, and curtain markers do not receive spoken-sentence IDs.
- Sentence IDs identify spoken/learnable English only.

UNIT ID RULE
U-A{act2}-S{scene2}-{sequence4}
- 0001 is PREAMBLE/context and has zero spoken sentence IDs.
- 0002 onward follows every explicit source speaker span in exact source order.
- The final unit is CURTAIN or QUICK_CURTAIN and has zero spoken sentence IDs.

SENTENCE ID RULE
SEN-{UNIT_ID}-{NN}
- NN starts at 01 within each dialogue unit.
- Only spoken sentences receive IDs.
- Stage directions and scene descriptions never receive Sentence IDs.
